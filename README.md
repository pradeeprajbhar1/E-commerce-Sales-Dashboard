# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales Dashboard

An interactive **E-Commerce Sales Dashboard** built using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze sales performance, profit, quantity, customers, products, payment methods, and geographical sales distribution.

The dashboard provides a clear and interactive view of business performance using KPIs, charts, filters, and geographical visualizations.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze e-commerce sales data and provide meaningful business insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

This dashboard helps businesses understand:

- Overall sales performance
- Profitability
- Product and category performance
- Customer contribution
- Payment method usage
- State-wise sales
- City-wise order distribution
- Monthly profit trends
- Sub-category performance

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Amount by State
Shows the total sales amount generated by different states.

Top-performing states include:

- Maharashtra
- Madhya Pradesh
- Uttar Pradesh
- Delhi

### 2. Quantity by Payment Mode
Shows the distribution of orders/quantity across different payment methods:

- COD
- UPI
- Debit Card
- Credit Card
- EMI

### 3. Profit by Sub-Category
Analyzes profit generated by different product sub-categories such as:

- Printers
- Bookcases
- Saree
- Accessories
- Tables

### 4. Orders by City
A geographical map showing the distribution of orders across different cities in India.

### 5. Amount by Customer Name
Displays sales contribution from the top customers.

### 6. Quantity by Category
Shows the quantity distribution across major product categories:

- Clothing
- Electronics
- Furniture

### 7. Profit by Month
Shows monthly profit trends and helps identify profitable and loss-making months.
